Telecom · RF Diagnostics

RAZUM

Decode the network. Live RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, PCI, and eNB/gNB identity across LTE, 5G NR, UMTS, and GSM — plus real event detection, dual-SIM support, Wi-Fi analysis, and RF-correlated speed testing.

What it reveals

Beyond the signal bar.

Most apps show you a number. RAZUM tells you what's actually happening on the network underneath it.

Live signal data

RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, RSSI, and Timing Advance across LTE, 5G NR, UMTS, and GSM, updating in real time with adaptive polling.

Cell & network identity

Serving and neighbour cell decode — PCI, eNB/gNB identity, PLMN, band, EARFCN, and PCI conflict detection.

Event detection

Handovers, radio link failures, and call technology — VoLTE, VoNR, VoWiFi, CS, with CSFB and SRVCC genuinely distinguished, not guessed.

Evidence-based call analysis

When a call ends abnormally, RAZUM correlates against the PDN bearer state to show whether the issue traces back to your own connection.

Dual-SIM & Wi-Fi

Full dual-SIM support, plus Wi-Fi network analysis alongside your cellular data — all in one view.

RF-correlated speed test

Run a speed test with the RF conditions that produced it captured alongside the result, not in isolation.

Session recording & playback

Record full sessions, replay them with a live metric scrubber, and export to CSV, KML, JSON, or a full HTML report.

Automatic reports

Scheduled PDF reports — daily, weekly, or monthly — summarising signal quality and notable events automatically.
