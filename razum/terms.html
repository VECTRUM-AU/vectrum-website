2. What RAZUM Is

It can record measurement sessions, detect network events, run network diagnostics and speed tests, and export what it has recorded.

3. Measurement Accuracy — Please Read

This is the most important section of these terms, and the one most likely to matter to you.

RAZUM is a diagnostic aid, not a calibrated instrument. It reports what your device's modem reports, decoded according to the relevant 3GPP specifications. It is not a substitute for professional drive-test equipment, and it must not be used as the sole basis for regulatory compliance, safety-of-life decisions, legal evidence, or network planning, acceptance or optimisation decisions.

Specifically, you acknowledge that:

  • The data originates with your device, not with us. RAZUM cannot read anything the modem does not expose. What is available differs by handset, chipset, firmware, Android version, carrier configuration and the permissions you have granted.
  • Some values are unavailable on some devices. Where a value is not reported, RAZUM is designed to say so rather than estimate it. A field marked unavailable or not reported means exactly that.
  • Decoded identifiers are derived, not measured. Values such as eNodeB, gNodeB and sector identifiers, frequency bands and channel numbers are calculated from what the modem supplies, using the standard formulas. Those formulas carry assumptions — the macro cell-identity split, the operator-configured gNB identifier length, and others — that are correct for most deployments and not for all. Where a configurable assumption exists, RAZUM exposes it as a setting.
  • Some figures are inferred and are labelled as such. Event classifications, quality estimates and any identity resolved from your own measurement history or from a cell file you have imported are interpretations, not direct readings.
  • Neighbour cell information is limited by design. A handset does not decode a neighbouring cell's system information during normal operation, so the detail available for neighbours is narrower than for the serving cell.
  • Speed test and diagnostic results reflect conditions at the moment of the test — including server load, the path across the internet and other traffic on your device — and are not a measurement of your operator's network in isolation.

We work to make RAZUM accurate and to correct it when it is wrong, but we do not warrant that any individual reading, decode, classification or export is correct, complete or fit for any particular purpose. If you intend to rely on a measurement for a decision that matters, verify it independently.
